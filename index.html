Creek Harbour

Discover a waterfront haven blending modern luxury, nature’s serenity, world-class marina, and extraordinary biodiversity nearby.

58+

Years of Experince

Dubai Creek Harbour is a vibrant waterfront community where luxury, nature, and innovation meet. With iconic amenities like Dubai Creek Marina and the nearby Ras Al Khor Wildlife Sanctuary, it offers a rare balance of modern living and natural tranquility.

Luxury Living,

Perfectly Connected

Dubai Creek Harbour ensures seamless connectivity with easy access to Downtown Dubai and Dubai International Airport. Residents enjoy picturesque promenades, world-class dining, schools, healthcare facilities, and the upcoming Dubai Metro expansion, making it a prime destination for both living and investment.

From elegant apartments and penthouses to luxury hotels like Vida Creek Harbour, Address Harbour Point, and Palace Residences, the community offers breathtaking views of the Creek and Dubai skyline. Residents and visitors alike can savor fine dining, relax at rooftop pools, and shop at premium retail outlets.

The Future Of Waterfront Living

Register Interest

Dubai Creek Harbour offers a tranquil lifestyle with wellness facilities, lively retail hubs, and stunning views of the marina and Dubai skyline. Enjoy outdoor dining, yoga at sunrise, or a leisurely stroll along the promenade. Capture vibrant creek views or take a boat tour to see iconic landmarks from the water. For an exceptional stay, check in at one of the luxury hotels or branded residences.

Relax by the infinity pool with Burj Khalifa views or explore the area on a bike. Whether you’re seeking a dream home or a solid investment, Dubai Creek Harbour offers exceptional value. Select from one to three bedroom apartments, spacious family homes, or luxury penthouses, each with modern design. Discover a new way of living at Dubai Creek Harbour.
